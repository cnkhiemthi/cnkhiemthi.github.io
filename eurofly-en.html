Eurofly

Eurofly is a flight simulator designed for blind and visually impaired users. Players can fly a variety of aircraft, take off, land, transport passengers, and explore airports around the world. Complete a wide range of flights with increasing levels of difficulty. Discover and download the game here.

Game Information

Last updated: July 23, 2026.

Operating System: Windows.

Supported: NVDA, JAWS, and SAPI 5.
